# Code of Conduct

ReClash welcomes people who participate in good faith, whether they contribute code, report a bug, improve a translation, or help another user.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and patient.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume mistakes before malice and ask for clarification when context is missing.
- Respect privacy. Do not repost private messages, logs, profiles, or personal information without permission.
- Keep discussions relevant to the project and make room for others to participate.
- Accept maintainer decisions about scope, moderation, and release readiness without harassment.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, spam, doxxing, and publishing another person's secrets or private data are not tolerated. The same applies to sustained bad-faith demands for support or attempts to turn project spaces into advertising for proxy providers.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, lock discussions, reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior harms the community. Serious conduct concerns may be reported privately to **hoxiee.dev@gmail.com**. Reports will be handled with discretion, but confidentiality cannot be guaranteed when action requires contacting the people involved.

This policy applies in repository spaces and in other public spaces when someone is representing ReClash.
